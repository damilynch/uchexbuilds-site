Scenario · Registration exception

A new intake appears to match an existing record.

A similar name and shared address appear in the system, but the identifiers have not been confirmed. The appointment is tomorrow.

What should you do next?

Why this matters

A possible duplicate is an exception—not proof of identity. Similar names or shared details are not enough to justify merging or deleting records.

Document what you checked, what remains uncertain, who owns the review and when follow-up is due. Do not guess or overwrite information.
