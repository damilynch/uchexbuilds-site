Know the workflow. Know what comes next.

An interactive web toolkit for remote healthcare support professionals and the practices that work with them.

For medical virtual assistants and patient access coordinators, the Hub will turn common administrative workflows into guided lessons, realistic practice activities and reusable job aids. For practices, it will provide structured onboarding and clearer expectations for remote support.

  • Patient intake, registration and coordination walkthroughs
  • Practice onboarding, task ownership and escalation guides
  • Scenario-based exercises, checklists and handoff resources
