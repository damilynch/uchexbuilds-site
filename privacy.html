Privacy Notice

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Draft for review before launch

This notice explains how UcheXBuilds ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this website or choose to join the product-update waitlist. Review and edit this draft to reflect the final tools, providers and practices before collecting visitor information.

Information you may provide

If you use the waitlist form, we ask for your email address and your agreement to receive product and launch updates. If you email us directly, we receive the information you include in your message.

How we use it

We use submitted details to send requested product updates, respond to enquiries and maintain the waitlist. We do not ask for patient information through this website.

Form provider

The waitlist form currently contains a placeholder Formspree endpoint. Before activating it, configure the form provider, review its privacy and retention terms, and update this notice with the provider's name and relevant details. The provider may process form submissions on our behalf under its own terms.

Analytics and cookies

No analytics script is active in this version. A commented Cloudflare Web Analytics snippet is included for optional setup. If analytics or other tracking tools are enabled, update this notice to explain what is used and how visitors can learn more.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website or deliver requested communications. Set and document an appropriate retention period before the waitlist goes live; delete information when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable obligations.

Your choices and requests

You may ask to correct or delete your waitlist information, or stop receiving updates, by emailing assist@uchexbuilds.site. Include a practical unsubscribe method in email updates once they begin.

Children

This website and toolkit are intended for professional and organisational audiences, not children.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to assist@uchexbuilds.site.

This is a plain-language working draft, not legal advice. Confirm applicable privacy requirements and actual data-handling arrangements before launch.
